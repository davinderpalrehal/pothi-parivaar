- (note) Product: Pothi Parivaar (Pothi=Book, Parivaar=Family) to catalog 1000+ physical and digital books.
- (direction) Audience: Kids (10, 7, 2) primary, parents secondary, Hermes agent (VPS) as automated advisor.
- (direction) Philosophy: Cultivate active reading, note-taking, and deep research habits; AI assists discovery, not replaces reading.
- (decision) Physical library: Flexible hierarchical location tracking (Location/Shelf), supporting incremental sorting by Author/Topic/Genre.
- (decision) Ingestion workflow: Dual-mode input — manual metadata entry (educational friction for kids) + prominent ISBN lookup (fast entry for parents/grandparents).
- (decision) Digital library: Track formats (Kindle, EPUB, PDF) alongside physical shelf locations.
- (decision) Profiles & Reading tracking: Single shared family catalog with lightweight reader assignment (who is reading, current page, start/finish date, re-read count).
- (decision) Hermes agent: Dedicated Hermes skill over REST API on VPS (search library, suggest books by criteria, check reading progress).
